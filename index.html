04  /  Pulmonary models, separated by evidence

Use lung labels.
Respect signal limits.

Only pulmonary outcomes belong in this project. A disease label must be paired with the same readings at the same time scale; unmatched hospital diagnoses, lung sounds, and derived symptom scores cannot fill that gap.

PLUMORING AI  /  PULMONARY EVIDENCE01  COPD FLARE FORECAST
TOLIFE V2.0 · 5-FOLD PARTICIPANT SPLIT

Forecast a COPD flare only in known COPD.

TOLIFE contains daily indices and clinically recorded moderate/severe COPD exacerbations. We tested seven-day HR/HRV, breathing, movement and SpO₂-like inputs; participant-held-out scores did not beat the no-skill reference. No model is deployed.

These are separate research tasks; none is a patient-facing diagnosis or validated ring alert. AAMOS-00 is retrospective asthma-worsening classification in adults already diagnosed with asthma. OSASUD labels apnea/hypopnea events during hospital sleep monitoring, not sleep-apnea diagnosis from this ring. COPD flare forecasts and event-window detection must not be presented as new disease diagnoses. No matched ring-reading pneumonia, bronchitis, or infection cohort was found. A separate 2026 COPD wearable study reported stronger results, but its participant data are restricted and those are the authors' results, not ours.
