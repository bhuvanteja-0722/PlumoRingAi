PLUMORING AI · PRIVACY

Your data stays
your own.

This software research prototype has no physical ring or live sensor connection. Its optional analysis view replays a compact, de-identified timeline from a public hospital dataset.

What this demo saves

Your selected ring finish and motion preference are saved in this browser’s local storage so the look and motion settings remain selected when you return. They stay in this browser and are not sent to a PlumoRing service by this page. Use Settings to change or reset them.

Sensor values and replay

The included monitoring timeline is a de-identified replay from the public PhysioNet Challenge 2019 dataset. The page does not ask for your name, account, or health records. If you use the optional local sequence endpoint, readings are sent only to the PlumoRing server running on your own computer. No live ring sensor stream is collected or stored.

Analytics and external services

The frontend does not include an analytics tracker or account system. It loads typefaces and 3D / animation code from public providers such as Google Fonts, unpkg, and esm.sh. Those providers and the site host may receive standard request metadata when your browser loads a page or asset; their own privacy terms apply.

Model and research limits

The experimental sequence model was trained on hospital vital signs and a sepsis benchmark label. It has not been validated on PlumoRing or smart-ring data, does not identify lung infections, and is not a diagnosis, calibrated clinical probability, severity estimate, or care recommendation. The displayed replay is hourly and recorded, not real-time ring monitoring.
