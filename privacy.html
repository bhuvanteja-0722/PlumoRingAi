This is a static concept frontend. The ring sensors and companion dashboard are illustrative; the page does not connect to a real ring or run a health model.

What this demo saves

Your selected ring finish and motion preference are saved in this browser’s local storage so the look and motion settings remain selected when you return. They stay in this browser and are not sent to a PlumoRing service by this page. Use Settings to change or reset them.

Sensor values and health information

Dashboard values, presets, and ring-part selections are sample interface data. This frontend does not ask for your name, account, or health records, and it does not submit those demo values to a model or backend.

Analytics and external services

The frontend does not include an analytics tracker or account system. It loads typefaces and 3D / animation code from public providers such as Google Fonts, unpkg, and esm.sh. Those providers and the site host may receive standard request metadata when your browser loads a page or asset; their own privacy terms apply.

Limits of this notice

This notice describes the current prototype frontend only. If the product later adds accounts, device sync, a trained model, or a backend, the data flows and this notice need to be reviewed and updated before those features are used with personal or health data.

Concept interface · no clinical output · no production health-data workflow is connected.
